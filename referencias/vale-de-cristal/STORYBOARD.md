## Prompts de movimento (image-to-video)
1. Slow, smooth aerial push-in toward the great city gate at golden hour. Thin mist drifts across the valley, red lion banners stir, waterfalls flow, a few birds glide across the frame, light glints on the crystal spires. One continuous camera move. Calm, majestic, slightly melancholic.
2. Slow dolly backward as the four walk toward camera on an ancient dusty stone road, golden dust glowing in backlight, cloaks moving in the breeze, rack focus from the commander's face to the three youths.
3. The tiny lizard glides down into frame with thin membranes stretched between its front and hind legs like a flying squirrel, lands on the commander's armored shoulder, and the membranes fold away out of sight; it tilts its head. Slow motion.
4. Slow lateral tracking shot through a busy medieval market; people move mechanically with tired, vacant faces, a vendor stares blankly, a child looks at the camera with a dull expression. Busy but strangely lifeless.
5. Slow tilt down onto a slightly murky river beside the stone wall; reflections ripple wrongly, a faint grey cloudiness swirls beneath, the light dims as a cloud passes. Subtle, ominous.
6. Near-static close-ups: Ravi turns a page of a worn notebook / Odara's eyes scan the market / Noá's hand tightens on his lion-pommel dagger as he looks up at the walls.
7. Hold on the commander's profile as the lizard on his shoulder turns its head to match his gaze, then a slow pull-back toward the far horizon: in the darkening blue mist on a distant ridge, barely visible, the silhouette of a massive bull-like beast of black volcanic rock with glowing molten cracks and pale golden horns, then it fades into the mist.
